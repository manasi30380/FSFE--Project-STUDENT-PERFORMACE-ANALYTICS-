Project Abstract
SLIDE 03 OF 06

One dashboard for academic progress

A proposed web application
Student Performance Analytics is a proposed web-based dashboard that will help teachers and students track academic performance in one place. It will record marks and attendance, compare subject-wise results and show progress across tests. Interactive charts and graphs will make strengths and weak areas easy to spot.
Marks

Test scores recorded for every student.

Attendance

Presence tracked and shown as a percentage.

Subject-wise performance

Results compared across subjects.

Academic progress

Growth shown from test to test.

SLIDE 04 OF 06

Why monitoring needs a better way

Today's challenges and how the proposed system will help
Manual tracking is slow

Recording marks and attendance by hand takes time.

→ The system will calculate and update them automatically.
Comparing subjects is hard

Finding strong and weak subjects needs extra effort.

→ Subject-wise charts will show them side by side.
Support can come late

Spotting students who need help takes extra work.

→ Low marks and attendance will be highlighted clearly.
